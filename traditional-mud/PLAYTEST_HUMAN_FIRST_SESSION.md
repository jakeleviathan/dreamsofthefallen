# Human First-Session Playtest

This sheet is for playing the Blackwall opening as a genuinely new player rather than as a developer checking features.

## Quality target

Target length: **20-30 minutes** from first entry into Cinder Ward to the first quiet pause at First-Mile Overlook.

The test is successful when the player understands how to move and interact, remembers at least one person from Blackwall, understands the first combat without opening a manual, notices and uses the first loot, understands what changed at level 2, and reaches the overlook wanting to choose what to do next.

Do not add content merely to fill the target time. Shorter is acceptable if the experience feels complete. Longer is a problem if the extra time comes from confusion, repeated reading, backtracking with no new information, or waiting for the game to explain itself.

## Stopwatch beats

| Beat | Desired elapsed time | What should be happening | Quit-risk question |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Orientation | 0-4 min | Cinder Ward, first typed commands, Mara becomes a recognizable person | Do I know what the game expects me to type? |
| Investigation | 4-10 min | readiness resolves, Ketta and the wagon turn routine work into a physical mystery | Am I curious, or am I doing chores because the text told me to? |
| First danger | 10-17 min | tracks lead below, Burrower fight teaches auto-attacks and one concrete class action | Do I understand what is happening quickly enough to make a choice? |
| Reward and discovery | 17-22 min | loot bracers, inspect/equip them, search nest/cache, bring Earth relic to Orrin | Did the fight produce something I care about, and did the mystery deepen? |
| Identity and release | 22-30 min | Sera, first step outside, level 2, First-Mile Overlook | Do I want to see what is beyond the next road without the game ordering me there? |

## Critical-path play

Play naturally and use LOOK whenever a new player plausibly would. The intended critical interactions are:

`NORTH` -> `TALK MARA` -> `EXAMINE SIGNAL BOARD` -> `DRILL SIGNALS` -> `REPORT READY`

Then return through Cinder Ward, meet Ketta, inspect and read the wagon damage, follow the sign east, descend, and `ATTACK BURROWER`.

During the fight, judge the combat text before opening HELP. Use the concrete class command the game suggests. After victory, `LOOT BURROWER`, `COMPARE SOOTSTEP HIDE BRACERS`, and `EQUIP SOOTSTEP HIDE BRACERS`, then continue with `SEARCH NEST`.

Search the hidden cache, return the handset to Orrin, go to Sera at the Demon Gate, step outside, and finally climb east to First-Mile Overlook.

## Record after each beat

Write down the actual elapsed time, any command you guessed incorrectly, any paragraph you skimmed, any point where you forgot why you were doing something, the first NPC name you remembered without looking it up, and the first moment that felt rewarding rather than instructional.

Mark a moment as **flat** if it works mechanically but produces no curiosity, tension, attachment, relief, or satisfaction. Mark a moment as **confusing** if the correct next action is only obvious because you already know the code. Mark a moment as **quit-risk** if a new player could reasonably decide the rest of the game will feel like that moment.

## Current polish decisions to protect

The first fight gives one concrete class action instead of dumping the ability list. The first Burrower loss is forgiving but clearly one-time. The first loot is explicit and useful. Level 2 names the actual newly unlocked class ability. The overlook creates no new emergency and no mandatory quest; it looks back at Mara, Ketta, Orrin, and Sera before pointing at the wider world.

When changing this slice, prefer cutting friction or sharpening a payoff over adding another tutorial step.
